Quill is a focused, adaptive read-later app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Capture anything worth reading later, keep the passages that matter, and sync cleanly to your Obsidian vault.

CAPTURE FAST
Paste a URL, share from Safari, or click from the macOS menu bar. Quill fetches the article's title, summary, and site name for you.

HIGHLIGHT WHAT MATTERS
Save passages with a one-line note. Quill tags them with the source URL so you always know where they came from.

INBOX · LIBRARY · HIGHLIGHTS
Three views: the queue for what you haven't read, the library for what you have, and Highlights for the lines you marked.

OBSIDIAN, NOTHING ELSE
Pick your vault. Quill writes one clean markdown file per article under `quill/articles/`, with YAML frontmatter. Your highlights become `> ` quote blocks you can read in any markdown viewer. A daily `quill/index.md` keeps the queue browsable in your vault.

DESIGN DETAILS
- Six built-in locales: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese
- Light / Dark / system theme
- Adaptive shell: tabs on iPhone, split on iPad, native menu-bar item on Mac
- Reading streak counter — gamification that doesn't get in the way

DESIGNED FOR SHARP USERS
- 100% offline, no tracking, no ads, no server
- Built with native SwiftUI + SwiftData
- iCloud sync toggle wired for v1.1

Quill is for readers who keep a second brain. If you write Obsidian notes, this is your inbox.
