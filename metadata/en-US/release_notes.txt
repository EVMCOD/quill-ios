v1.0 — Initial release

A read-later and highlights app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac with bidirectional Obsidian sync and macOS menu-bar capture.

What's inside:
- Capture URLs from any app, share sheet, or the menubar (⌘N on Mac)
- Per-article highlights with one-line notes
- Inbox · Library · Highlights views
- Reading streak counter
- Light / Dark / system theme picker
- Six locales: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese

Obsidian sync:
- `quill/articles/<slug>-<id>.md` — one file per article with YAML frontmatter
- `quill/index.md` — your reading queue
- Highlights become `> ` quote blocks with optional notes
- Folder picker with security-scoped bookmark
